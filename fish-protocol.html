Part One

Day 1

Pretreatment

  1. Incubate slides in the oven.

    20 min70°C
Deparaffinization

  1. Incubate in xylene — 3 changes.

    3 × 5 min

  2. Incubate in 100% ethanol — 2 changes.

    2 × 3 min

  3. Completely air dry sections.

  4. Incubate in pre-warmed FISH pretreatment solution PT-1 buffer, 1×.

    35 min98°C

  5. Wash in 2× SSC wash buffer at RT.

    5 min

  6. Wash in deionized or distilled water at RT — 2 changes.

    2 × 1 min

  7. Apply pepsin solution dropwise to the specimen and incubate in a humidity chamber.

    10–30 min37°C

  8. Wash in 2× SSC wash buffer at RT.

    5 min

  9. Wash in deionized or distilled water at RT — 2 changes.

    2 × 1 min

  10. Dehydrate through an ethanol series: 70%, 90%, 100%.

    5 min each

  11. Completely air dry sections.

    30 min

  12. Pipette 10 µL of the FISH probe onto each pretreated specimen.

  13. Cover specimens with a 22 × 22 mm coverslip and seal.

  14. Seal the sides of the coverslip with rubber cement.

  15. Denature on a hot plate or hybridizer, then hybridize in a humidity chamber.

    Denature 5 min75°CHybridize 16 hr37°C
Part Two

Day 2

  1. Soak slide in 2× SSC at RT to loosen the coverslip.

    Do not pull off the coverslip — let it float free.

  2. Wash in 2× SSC + 50 µL IGEPAL.

    2 min

  3. Wash in 2× SSC + 150 µL IGEPAL.

    2 min72°C

  4. Wash with MilliQ water.

    2 min

  5. Dehydrate through an alcohol series: 70%, 90%, 100%.

    1 min each

  6. Air dry.

  7. Mount using DAPI with antifade reagent, then seal the sides with clear nail (gel) polish.

  8. Visualize using a fluorescence microscope.
